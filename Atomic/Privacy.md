Privacy is the right of individuals to control how their personal information is collected, used, processed, and shared by systems and organizations.

Privacy goes beyond technical confidentiality: it concerns individual control, consent, and data governance over personal and sensitive information.

In formal security frameworks such as Common Criteria, privacy can be expressed through properties including:

* **Anonymity**: Preventing an action from being linked to a specific individual.
* **Unlinkability**: Preventing multiple actions or pieces of information from being associated with the same individual.
* **Unobservability**: Preventing others from determining whether a particular action or communication is taking place.

Core idea: Privacy is about maintaining control over personal information and limiting how an individual's identity or activities can be observed, associated, or used.
